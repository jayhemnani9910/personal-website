  keyDecisions:
    - decision: "TimescaleDB over plain Postgres"
      reasoning: "25 years of daily ticks across 10 tickers plus intraday macro series is time-series-heavy; TimescaleDB hypertables keep range queries fast while staying SQL-native."
      tradeoff: "One more extension to operate versus reaching for a dedicated TSDB like InfluxDB and losing relational joins to the dimension tables."
    - decision: "Batch-and-flush Kafka consumer, not per-message inserts"
      reasoning: "Upserting in batches of 50 (or every 10s, whichever comes first) cuts write amplification and keeps the warehouse current without hammering it."
      tradeoff: "A small bounded staleness window in exchange for far fewer transactions."
    - decision: "Incremental DAGs keyed on last-loaded date"
      reasoning: "Each ETL run reads the latest date already in the warehouse and only fetches the gap, so reruns are idempotent and backfills are cheap."
      tradeoff: "More bookkeeping per DAG than a naive full reload."

# Overview
Unified batch + streaming market data warehouse tracking 10 major US equities with 25 years of history. Combines 4 data sources (yfinance prices, SEC EDGAR filings, FRED macro indicators, and live Kafka streams) through ~18 Airflow DAGs into an 8-table star schema on TimescaleDB. Fully containerized with 7 Docker services.
